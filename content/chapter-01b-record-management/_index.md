Everything FortiSOAR does -- every playbook, every connector call, every ticket -- starts and ends
with a **record**. Before you automate anything, you need to be fluent in the data model the
automation acts on.

This chapter is short and entirely hands-on. You will create a record by hand, read it, and see
FortiSOAR's audit trail record what you did -- including the automation that fired the moment you
hit Save.
