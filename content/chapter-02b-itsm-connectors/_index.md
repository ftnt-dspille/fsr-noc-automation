FortiSOAR only orchestrates what it can reach. In the previous chapter you connected it to the
Fortinet side of the house -- FortiGate and FortiManager. This chapter connects it to the systems
that sit **around** the network: your ticketing system and your cloud asset inventory.

By the end you will have two working, health-checked connections that later chapters build on:
**ServiceNow**, which every use case ending in "…and open a ticket" depends on, and **FortiCloud
Asset Management**, which answers what you own and when its support expires.
